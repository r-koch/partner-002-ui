paiir.me

Compatibility first. Privacy by design.

Most dating apps optimize for swiping on photos. paiir.me helps you find people who actually fit your life and expectations — without giving up your privacy or wasting your time.
1. Setup
You answer a short core questionnaire about yourself and what you are looking for, weight how much each topic matters (little to very), and mark red flags. You pick a handle from our curated list, and you can use a portrait photo to generate an avatar on your device — or use the default avatar.
2. Candidates
Devices compare core questionnaire criteria and calculate a compatibility score. Anyone not excluded by a red flag you or they set appears in your Candidates list. The higher the score, the closer they fit what you are looking for — and you have full control over filtering and sorting the list.
3. Introductions
If a Candidate looks promising, select Introduce. If they do the same, you enter an Introduction together. Now you can see their avatar and get to know them by asking optional questions or starting a visual Warm-up. If you ask a question you haven't answered yet, you'll be prompted to answer it first.
4. Pairings
If the Introduction goes well and you want to talk, select paiir.me. Once both of you do, you become a Pairing and unlock free-text chat — with optional conversation suggestions based on your Introduction if you ever get stuck. Becoming a Pairing uses one ticket per side (only consumed when both select paiir.me; 3 free tickets per month, additional tickets from EUR 0.99).
5. Privacy & on-device design
Your personal data stays on your device. Your avatar is generated on your device from a photo that never leaves it, and information is exchanged peer-to-peer only with the people you choose.
