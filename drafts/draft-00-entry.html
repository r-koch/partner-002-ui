This is the full interactive draft. The journey: sign in with a provider, complete setup (age, terms, privacy, consents, avatar, name), answer 16 required questions, optionally add more info, then discover — where you can match, enter a "Getting to know" stage (optional info, ice breakers, requests), and finally chat. Every page is a functional sketch: buttons navigate or demo their state. Start at the login screen, or use the arrows at the bottom to jump anywhere.
