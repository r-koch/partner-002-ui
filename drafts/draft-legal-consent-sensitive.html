Consent for sensitive answers

Version 1 · 2026-10-25
This is the text you agree to on the setup screen. It covers one purpose and nothing else.

1. The exact wording

I agree that my answers — including those that may reveal sensitive information such as religion, political opinion or sexual orientation — may be used for the purpose of finding compatible users and being found by them. I can withdraw this consent at any time.

2. What this authorises

One purpose only: comparing your answers with other people's answers so that compatible people are shown to each other. That is the entire scope.

3. What it does not authorise

  • No advertising, no marketing, no behaviour analytics.
  • No profiling beyond the compatibility comparison itself.
  • No sharing or selling to anyone, and no use in moderation decisions.
  • No use for training any model.

4. Legal basis

Religion, political opinion and sexual orientation are special-category data under Art. 9 GDPR. We rely on your explicit consent under Art. 9(2)(a), and on your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) for the comparison itself. Without that consent we could not use these answers at all — which is why we ask before you answer them, and why the answers are optional.

5. Where the data is

On your device, encrypted. We hold no copy. The comparison happens between devices and reveals only how much overlaps, never which answers — see the privacy notice.

6. Withdrawing

Withdraw any time in Settings → Consent, or simply delete the answer. The consequence is limited to what it should be: that answer no longer counts for compatibility, and the rest of the app works exactly as before. No account is closed, no feature is locked, no advantage is lost.

7. How long

Until you withdraw or delete the answer — and never on our servers in any case.

Controller and contact: see the imprint. You may complain to the Austrian Data Protection Authority at any time.
