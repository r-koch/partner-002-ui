Consent for sensitive answers

Version 2 · draft · 2026-10-31
This consent covers the 16 required answers and optional answers that may reveal sensitive information, if you choose to provide them. Legal review of the combined consent is still open.

1. The exact wording

I explicitly consent to paiir.me using my required answers and any optional answers that may reveal sensitive information, such as my religion, political views, sexual orientation or intimacy preferences, so it can find compatible users and let them find me.

2. What this authorises

One purpose only: use the 16 required answers, plus any optional answers that may reveal sensitive information if you choose to provide them, to find compatible users and let them find you. The comparison runs between the two users' devices; we do not receive the full answer set. The comparison may reveal topics where you agree, including the shared value, and topic names where you differ. See the privacy notice.

Optional intimacy questions remain voluntary. Whether this one consent is sufficiently specific for them remains under legal review.

3. What it does not authorise

  • No advertising, no marketing, no behaviour analytics.
  • No profiling beyond the compatibility comparison itself.
  • We do not sell your answers or send full answer sets to other users. Compatibility results can show agreed topics and their shared values, and the names of topics where answers differ. This consent does not authorise moderation use.
  • No use for training any model.

4. Legal basis

Religion, political views, sexual orientation and some intimacy answers may be special-category data under Art. 9 GDPR. Our intended basis is your explicit consent under Art. 9(2)(a), and your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) for the compatibility comparison. The 16 required answers are needed to use Paiir; without this consent, you cannot create or use an account. Optional answers remain optional, but this consent is intended to cover any optional answers that may reveal sensitive information. Legal review of whether consent is freely given when service access depends on it, and sufficiently specific for optional sensitive answers, is still open. This draft is not ready to publish.

5. Where the data is

On your device, encrypted. We hold no copy. The comparison happens between devices: you learn how much overlaps and which topics you agree on (with the shared value), while on differing topics only the topic name is shown — never their answer set. See the privacy notice.

6. Withdrawing

You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Consents & permissions. The app deletes all answers on this device immediately and disables Paiir until you consent again. Your account stays registered until you delete it.

7. How long

Your answers stay encrypted on your device; we never store them on our servers. When you withdraw consent, the app deletes all answer data from that device immediately. Your account row remains until you delete the account.

Controller and contact: see the imprint. You may complain to the Austrian Data Protection Authority at any time.
