Privacy notice

Version 3 · draft · 2026-11-03
Short version: your answers, your avatar and your chat never reach our servers unless you attach an avatar or message to a moderation report. We hold a small account row on our servers, and nothing else. Consent for required and optional sensitive answers, and the legal basis for lifetime moderation counters and closed-account guards, remain under legal review.

1. Who is responsible

The controller for the processing described here is the operator named in the imprint. Questions about privacy, and requests under this notice, go to the contact given there.

2. What stays on your device

  • Your answers, including all 17 required and 28 optional questions — encrypted on your device, with no server copy and no backup.
  • Your source photo, which never leaves your device. Only the stylized avatar derived from it is transmitted device to device in Introductions and Pairings after mutual interest (or to us if a recipient reports that avatar).
  • Your chat messages — end-to-end encrypted plain text; we cannot read them and do not store them unless a participant submits a message in a moderation report.

3. What we hold on the server

The account row and moderation records, in full:

FieldWhy
Identity provider + account numberSo it is you when you come back, and to block re-registration if an account is Closed for abuse

4. Why we process what we hold, and on what basis

Account row, pairwise blocks and pairing tickets: performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Moderation case records, lifetime Confirmed report counts, deterrent pricing tiers, and the Closed-account provider guard: our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe and deterring abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; legal review of proportionality and retention is open). Receipts: legal obligation to keep accounting records (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR). Sensitive answers: your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), see section 5.

5. Sensitive answers

The 17 required questions drive compatibility; some answers can reveal religion, political views or sexual orientation. We ask for explicit consent to use those answers and any optional answers that may reveal sensitive information if you choose to provide them; without it you cannot create or use Paiir. Optional questions remain voluntary. The two users' devices compare the answers; the full answer set is not sent to our servers. The comparison may show topics where you agree, including shared values, and topic names where you differ; see section 6. We do not use these answers for advertising, analytics or profiling beyond compatibility. You may withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Consents & permissions; all answers on this device are deleted immediately and Paiir is disabled until you consent again. Your account remains until you delete it. Legal review of freely given and sufficiently specific consent, including for optional sensitive answers, is still open; this draft is not ready to publish.

6. What a comparison shows

Your device compares your answers with the other person's device. You see the result as a score, plus the topics where you agree — including the value you share, such as both wanting children — and the topics where you differ, by name only. You never see their answer on a topic where you differ, and you never see their full answer set. We do not store the comparison.

7. Payments and receipts

A certified payment provider (or your app store) handles the payment; we never see your card or bank details. At web purchase, the payment provider tells us the email address you used there, and we keep it for one purpose only: sending the receipt and meeting our accounting duty. It is not part of your profile.

8. Who else is involved

We use a small number of processors, each bound by a data-processing agreement:

  • your identity provider (Google, Apple, Microsoft or LinkedIn) — login only;
  • the payment provider — web payment and receipts;
  • our hosting provider, in the European Union — the server records in section 3;
  • the app stores — distribution and, if you buy there, their own billing.

No other recipients, except competent law-enforcement or child-safety authorities when required by law in severe moderation cases. No transfers outside the EU/EEA unless bound by EU standard contractual clauses.

9. How long

Your account row and lifetime Confirmed reports count live while your account exists. Detailed moderation case metadata (reason, dates, decision notes) is deleted after 12 months, and report attachments are deleted when the case and appeal window close unless a legal hold applies. When you delete an Active account, your account row is deleted and your used handle stays retired; while an account is Suspended, account deletion is disabled until the appeal or 28-day window finishes. If an account moves to Closed after a Confirmed (or unappealed) suspension report, we delete the profile state and retain only the provider identity guard (OIDC issuer and subject) to prevent re-registration with that provider (retention period under legal review). Receipts are kept for the statutory accounting period (7 years in Austria). Your answers, source photo and unattached chat are not stored by us at all.

11. Complaints

You can complain to the Austrian Data Protection Authority, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, dsb.gv.at — or to the supervisory authority where you live.

12. What we never do

We do not sell your answers or send other users your full answer set. Compatibility results may show topics you agree on, including shared values, and topic names where you differ; see section 6. We do not run advertising or behaviour analytics on your answers, or build marketing profiles.

13. Changes

If this notice changes in a way that matters, we show you the change before it applies.
