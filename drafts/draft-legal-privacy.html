Privacy notice

Version 1 · 2026-10-25
Short version: your answers, your avatar and your chat never reach our servers. We hold an account row that is small enough to print below, and nothing else.

1. Who is responsible

The controller for the processing described here is the operator named in the imprint. Questions about privacy, and requests under this notice, go to the contact given there.

2. What never leaves your device

  • Your answers, including all required and optional questions — encrypted, with no server copy and no backup.
  • Your photo, and the avatar derived from it. Only the drawn avatar is transmitted, and only device to device.
  • Your chat messages — end-to-end encrypted; we cannot read them and do not store them.

3. What we hold on the server

The account row, in full:

FieldWhy
Identity provider + account numberSo it is you when you come back
Your handleSo people can recognise you; never reissued
Created dateAge of the account, security
Pairing credit balanceSo a purchase is honoured
Report countersSo repeated abuse can be recognised

No name, no email address, no birth date, no photo, no answers, no messages.

4. Why we process what we hold, and on what basis

Account row and pairing credit: performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Report counters: our legitimate interest in keeping the service usable and stopping abuse (Art. 6(1)(f)), balanced against your interest — the counters contain no content. Receipts: legal obligation to keep accounting records (Art. 6(1)(c)). Sensitive answers: your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)), see section 5.

5. Sensitive answers

Religion, politics and orientation are special-category data. We ask for your explicit consent before you answer them, and we use them for exactly one purpose: comparing your answers with someone else's to find compatible people. We never use them for advertising, analytics or profiling of any other kind. Every one of these answers is optional, and you can withdraw consent at any time by deleting the answer — compatibility then continues without it.

6. Comparing answers without seeing them

Your device compares your answers with the other person's device. What travels is how much overlaps, never which answers. We cannot reconstruct an answer from it, and we do not keep the comparison.

7. Payments and receipts

A certified payment provider handles the payment; we never see your card details. At purchase, the provider tells us the email address you used there, and we keep it for one purpose only: sending the receipt and meeting our accounting duty. It is not used for anything else and it is not part of your profile.

8. Who else is involved

We use a small number of processors, each bound by a data-processing agreement:

  • your identity provider (Google, Apple, Microsoft or LinkedIn) — login only;
  • the payment provider — payment and receipts;
  • our hosting provider, in the European Union — the account row;
  • the app stores — distribution and, if you buy there, their own billing.

No other recipients. No transfers outside the EU/EEA unless the processor is bound by EU standard contractual clauses.

9. How long

The account row lives until you delete your account. Receipts are kept for the statutory accounting period (7 years in Austria). Report counters are kept while the account exists and deleted with it. Your answers, avatar and chat are not stored by us at all, so there is no retention period for them.

11. Complaints

You can complain to the Austrian Data Protection Authority, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, dsb.gv.at — or to the supervisory authority where you live.

12. What we never do

We do not sell or share your data, we do not run advertising or behaviour analytics on your answers, we do not build marketing profiles, and we do not ask for permissions we do not need.

13. Changes

If this notice changes in a way that matters, we show you the change before it applies.
