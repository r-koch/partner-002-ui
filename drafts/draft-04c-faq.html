Short answers, grouped. Every ? in the app opens one of these.
Getting in
Why do you ask my age?We ask once, on your way in. It is a self-declaration and we do not check proof. Adults only is our own rule, and the app stores require it for an app like this.
Which providers can I sign in with?Google, Apple, Microsoft or LinkedIn. All use OpenID Connect: the provider handles the login and we only store a provider account number — never your email, name or profile. One account belongs to one provider identity.
Can someone take my handle after I change it?Handles come from our two word lists: yours may be changed once per week, and a handle that has been used is never reissued — not after a change and not after an account is deleted.
Privacy
How does paiir.me work?You answer questions about yourself and what you are looking for. Only those answers affect compatibility; red flags are hard exclusions. Your answers stay encrypted on your device, with no server copy or backup. No real photos of you: a photo-derived avatar is shared peer-to-peer after mutual interest.
Why do you need sensitive answers at all?Religion, politics and orientation are special-category data, so we ask for explicit consent first. They are used for one purpose only: matching. Nothing else, ever — no advertising, no analytics, no sharing. Skip any of them; they are optional.
Who can see my answers?Only people you are paired with, and only stage by stage. Your interest stays private until both people declare one.
Where does my photo go?Nowhere. Your photo never leaves your phone. The avatar is drawn on your device from it, and only the drawn avatar is sent — directly to people you both chose. We never hold it and we never show a real photo.
Are there real photos on the platform?No. Nobody's real photo is ever uploaded, stored or shown. You see drawn avatars, and they travel device to device.
Is my chat private?End-to-end encrypted, text only — no images no attachments. We cannot read it and we do not store it.
What happens if I lose my phone?Your answers exist only on that device, encrypted, with no server copy and no backup — so a lost, reset or broken phone means you answer again from scratch. A handle that was taken stays retired regardless.
Answers and matching
How is the candidate list sorted?By how well each person fits what you are looking for. Score is value only — higher is better. There is no cut-off: keep scrolling as far as you like. Only red flags and the ranges you set remove someone.
Why does the score never change?It is fixed at pairing time. Later answers do not rewrite it.
What is a red flag?A hard boundary: a person you pair with can never break it. It is kept private and never shown to them.
What is required and what is optional?16 required questions drive matching. Everything else — interests, personality, extended and deep questions — is voluntary, answerable later, and never required to browse, be browsed or pair.
When do I answer the extra questions?On demand: when someone asks one, or on your own to unlock asking it yourself. They are answered on your device, edited any time, and nothing there blocks you from pairing.
Why is one answer green and another not?Green means you both answered the same. You only see an answer of theirs that you have answered yourself — otherwise answer yours first, then you can ask.
Is my personality type used for matching?No. It is shown to people after you pair, as context for getting to know each other. Only the required questions are used for matching.
Do my interests affect who I see?No. Interests — imported or picked by hand — are conversation material only. They never change matching.
How precise is my location?You state it; it is never detected from your device. You pick a distance band: under 15 km, same city, same region, or anywhere. Only the matched band ever leaves your phone — never a pin or a postcode.
Stages and pairing
What can I see once we are paired?All their answered optional info is open to read, and anything unanswered can be asked — after you have answered it yourself. Warm-ups, conversation starters and the activity log live here. Moving to chat needs both of you to opt in.
Can I change my pick later?No. A choice is permanent and moves you to the next pair; the dots let you review earlier pairs with your picks kept as they were. Results appear after you both finish.
Can I see who is interested in me?Not before you declare interest yourself. Declaring is one-sided and private; it is revealed only when both people have declared one.
Money
What does it cost?Matching and getting to know someone are free forever. Opening the chat costs an advance on each side: both of you need one when you declare, and the system checks both. The advance is reserved for that pairing until you both declare, and released if either of you withdraws or the declaration lapses. A certified payment provider handles the card; we never see the number.
Your account
What happens when I sign out?Signing out removes this device only. Your answers stay on it, encrypted; nothing is deleted from anywhere else.
What happens if I delete my account?Server records, pairings and chat state are removed. Handles are never reused, and your answers disappear with the device.
