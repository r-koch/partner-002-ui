Refunds and withdrawal

Version 1 · 2026-10-25
Short version: 14 days, no reason needed, no questions asked. After 14 days you get back what you have not used.

1. What can be bought

Pairing credits, which unlock the chat with one person, and a subscription for a fixed period. Prices are shown before you buy. Payment is handled by a certified payment provider; we never see your card details.

2. Fourteen days, no reason needed

For 14 days after a purchase you can withdraw it — by law, and by our own rule. You do not have to give a reason, and we do not ask whether the statutory exception for already-started digital services applies. One tap in Settings → Payments, or a short message to the contact in the imprint, is enough.

3. What happens then

We refund to the same payment method within 14 days of your withdrawal. It is not a second payment in the other direction: it is an adjustment to the payment you made, for any amount up to it, so it creates no new charge for either of us. The only money we cannot return is the fee our payment provider keeps from the original payment (roughly 0.25 EUR plus 1.5% of it).

The refund is made in the currency you paid, so if your bank converts it, the figure on your statement can differ slightly — that is your bank's rate, not a deduction by us. If you paid through an app store, the store handles the refund under its own rules: it decides the amount and cannot issue a partial refund on our behalf.

4. How the amount is worked out

You get back the price you actually paid for the credits you have not used, minus the payment fee described above. No other deduction is made, and there is no refund fee.

Example: a 5-credit pack for 4 EUR, two credits used, three unused. You paid 2.40 EUR for those three, so 2.40 EUR comes back, minus the payment fee of about 0.31 EUR — 2.09 EUR. The same calculation applies after the first 14 days: unused credits stay refundable, which is our own promise and not only the statutory minimum.

5. What has already been used

A credit you have spent to open a chat is a service already performed, so it is not refundable after the 14 days. The chat itself stays open — a refund never closes a conversation you are in.

6. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy shortens the rights you have under Austrian and EU consumer law, including warranty rights and your right to withdraw under the FAGG. If this document and the law disagree, the law wins.

7. If something goes wrong

Write to the contact in the imprint first — that resolves almost everything faster than any formal route. You keep the right to go to court or to the competent consumer authority.
