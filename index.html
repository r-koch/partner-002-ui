    How you get to know each other

    Getting to know someone happens in steps. You both say yes to each one — no one can push the next step on the other.

    • 1 Match You both said yes. That's all either of you knows right now. where you are

      Right now the only new thing is that the interest was mutual. Nothing else changed hands — no names, no photos, no way to reach each other.

    • 2 More answers Swap your Extended & Deep answers and pick a few conversation starters. ready when you are

      Your Extended and Deep answers — the optional tiers you can opt into from your profile — become shareable here, after a mutual match. Plus tidy prompts like "ask about their free time." Structured only — no free text. (Same answers, two uses: they're both your profile detail and this rung's reveal material.)

    • 3 Photos Swap photos when you're both ready. coming later

      Not built yet. It needs care and a few checks first — photos are sensitive, so this step is still in the works. No hard feelings; it'll be here when it's ready.

    • 4 Names & contact Trade names or a way to reach each other. The last step. coming later

      Also not built yet — it needs the most care of all, plus a clear plan for how you'll share details safely. It's the finish line, not something to rush.

    Any step you've both unlocked stays unlockable — you can walk it back whenever. Unmatching is quiet and both-sided: no one gets told who ended it.

    Before you join

    Three separate things, on purpose: the terms you agree to, the privacy notice you read, and the consents you explicitly opt into. Agreeing to the terms never counts as consent.

    1 · Terms of use draft
    • Beta. This is a prototype in testing, not a finished product. No promise of outcomes, matches, or that anything will work a certain way.
    • Adults only. This service is for adults (18+). The exact age-policy wording and any verification are to be confirmed with counsel.
    • Conduct. Be honest in your answers. No harassment, no impersonation, no abuse. You can unmatch or report anyone, anytime.
    • Reporting. Reports go to a human reviewer. Demo only — nothing is actually reviewed here.
    • Service limits. The oversharing filter is best-effort, not a guarantee. No photos, names, or contact details are part of this demo.

    This is a draft, not final legal terms, and it awaits counsel.

    Data categoriesQuestionnaire answers (which may include special-category data such as sexual orientation where you answer that); optional local context import (e.g. Google Takeout, parsed on-device); derived matching signals.

    About privacy

    This is a demo, not a real product. The match score is a preference match — how much your answers line up — not a chemistry or compatibility prediction. It doesn't predict who you'll click with. Your answers and imports stay on this device; only simple labels and counts ever move around. Everything you share is your choice, and you can see, edit, or remove any of it. Red flags are private dealbreakers — someone you flag simply never shows up, and they're never told. The filter that stops accidental oversharing is best-effort — it's not a promise nothing can ever leak. Made-up data only, no real people.
