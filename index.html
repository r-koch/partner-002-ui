Your profile

Answer a few questions. Each one is marked About you (who you are) or Looking for (what you want in a partner) — some ask both. Everything here is yours to see and edit.

👥 About you = an attribute of yours (your gender, height, habits). 🎯 Looking for = what you seek in someone else. ⚑ red flags live here only.
Core needed — about 4 min

On any Looking for answer, tap the little ⚑ to make it a red flag: people who answer that way never show up, and they're never told why. It's private. (There are no flags on About you answers — that side just describes you.)

Extended optional — ~15 questions, ~4 min

Values & lifestyle details. You can add (or remove) this anytime from your profile — the same answers also become available after a mutual match (see the reveal ladder).

Other Extended questions — values + lifestyle

Ambition, quiet vs. loud, spending style, family, humor, how you show you care, how you talk, drinking, exercise, food, politics in detail, how much work matters, travel, downtime, ideal weekend.

Deep optional — ~12 questions, ~4 min

Nuanced compatibility. Same deal — add anytime, and these answers also unlock after a mutual match.

Deep questions — nuanced compatibility

Attachment style, how you argue, life outlook, money, space, feelings, where you want to live, health, social media, dealbreakers, first date, life pace.

Answering more doesn't change what anyone sees. Cards stay lean either way.

    How you get to know each other

    Getting to know someone happens in steps. You both say yes to each one — no one can push the next step on the other.

    • 1 Match You both said yes. That's all either of you knows right now. where you are

      Right now the only new thing is that the interest was mutual. Nothing else changed hands — no names, no photos, no way to reach each other.

    • 2 More answers + cartoon avatars Swap your Extended & Deep answers and share stylized cartoon avatars. ready when you are

      Your Extended and Deep answers — the optional tiers you can opt into from your profile — become shareable here, after a mutual match. Plus tidy prompts like "ask about their free time." Structured only — no free text. (Same answers, two uses: they're both your profile detail and this rung's reveal material.)

      Cartoon avatars are a stylized non-identifying likeness drawn on your device — real photos never appear here; sharing real images happens only out-of-band after direct chat is unlocked. Each cartoon is algorithm-signed (e.g. cartoonizer/v1 | input:3f9a2c11) so it's provably a stylized render, never a raw photo.

      Try it: pick a local image and it's cartoonized on this device — the file is never uploaded or stored; only the stylized cartoon is drawn here.

    • 3 Names & contact Trade names or a way to reach each other. The last step. coming later

      Not built yet — it needs the most care, plus a clear plan for how you'll share details safely. It's the finish line, not something to rush. Real photos are never part of the ladder: they stay out-of-band after direct chat is unlocked.

    Any step you've both unlocked stays unlockable — you can walk it back whenever. Unmatching is quiet and both-sided: no one gets told who ended it.

    Avatar demo

    A stylized line-art avatar from a face — run on this device with the MediaPipe Face Landmarker and a deterministic line-art renderer (ink strokes on a light paper background, no flat-color face fill). This is a browser prototype of the production phone path.

    Try it

    Grant camera access, or skip it — a bundled public-domain test portrait (a NASA photo, no real user photo) runs through the same pipeline.

    processed on your device only — nothing leaves your phone; no face data is kept.

    Preparing the on-device landmarker…

    How it works

    Your camera frame (or the bundled test portrait) goes to the on-device Face Landmarker, which returns a transient face mesh — no identity embedding, no recognition. That mesh is used to add mesh-driven strokes on top of a deterministic line-art re-render (Sobel luminance edges drawn as ink lines on a light paper background; no flat-color fill inside the face). Only the finished line-art portrait is shown. The raw frame and the mesh are discarded immediately — nothing is stored, nothing is uploaded. (This demo runs MediaPipe in the browser; the production path would use the MediaPipe Tasks Android SDK on the device.)

    Also see the default avatar — the bright, androgynous outlined silhouette everyone gets by default.

    The default avatar

    Everyone starts here: a bright, positive, androgynous outline — no discernible features, no photo, no likeness. It is drawn entirely from code, no image assets.

    No eyes, no nose, no mouth, no hair, no gender cues — just a warm, positive outlined head and shoulders. It reads as a person without reading as anyone in particular.

    About privacy

    This is a demo, not a real product. The match score is a preference match — how much your answers line up — not a chemistry or compatibility prediction. It doesn't predict who you'll click with. Your answers and imports stay on this device; only simple labels and counts ever move around. Everything you share is your choice, and you can see, edit, or remove any of it. Red flags are private dealbreakers — someone you flag simply never shows up, and they're never told. The filter that stops accidental oversharing is best-effort — it's not a promise nothing can ever leak. Made-up data only, no real people.
