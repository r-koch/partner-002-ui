Terms of use

1. What paiir.me is

paiir.me helps adults find people whose expectations fit their own. It screens for compatibility. It does not predict chemistry and it does not promise you will meet anyone.

2. Your account

You sign in with an identity provider (Google, Apple, Microsoft, Facebook, LinkedIn or PayPal). We store only the provider and your anonymous provider ID — never your email address, name or profile. One account belongs to one provider identity.

You must be 18 or older. We do not verify age; the declaration is yours, and we close accounts we learn are under 18.

Your handle comes from our curated word lists and can be changed at most once every 7 days. A handle that has been used is never reissued.

3. Your answers

Your answers are stored only on your device, encrypted. We keep no server copy and no backup. If you lose, reset or break your device, your answers are gone and you start again.

Devices compare core questionnaire criteria and calculate a compatibility score. You see the score and which topics you agree on (including the value you share); on topics where you differ you see only the topic name, never their answer. Their full answer set is never shown.

4. Avatars

You use either our default avatar or an avatar generated on your device from a portrait photo that never leaves your device. Real photos are never uploaded or sent. Your avatar is shared directly between devices only in Introductions and Pairings, after mutual interest. You can change or regenerate your avatar at any time.

You may not include contact information in an avatar, use another person's likeness without permission, or redistribute another user's avatar outside the service.

5. Candidates, Introductions and Pairings

Selecting Introduce on a Candidate is private until both people select it. Mutual Introduce opens an Introduction (always free), where you see each other's avatars, ask optional questions (if you ask a question you haven't answered yet, you answer it first), and play visual Warm-ups.

Selecting paiir.me in an Introduction reserves one pairing ticket on your side. Once both of you select paiir.me, you become a Pairing and unlock private text chat, with optional conversation suggestions based on your Introduction. Either side can end an Introduction or Pairing at any time.

6. Chat

Chat opens inside a Pairing once both sides select paiir.me. It is end-to-end encrypted plain text only — no images, no attachments and no clickable links. We cannot read it and we do not store it.

7. Pairing tickets and pricing

While your account has zero Confirmed reports, three pairings a month are free and do not roll over (resetting at 00:00 UTC on the 1st of each calendar month). After your first Confirmed report, free pairings permanently end.

Each Pairing requires one ticket per side. Neither ticket is consumed until both people select paiir.me; if the other person does not opt in within 14 days, the reservation lapses and your ticket returns. Paid tickets never expire.

To deter abuse, your ticket price depends on your lifetime count of Confirmed reports that did not result in account suspension:

  • 0 Confirmed reports: EUR 0.99 (base)
  • 1 Confirmed report: EUR 1.99
  • 2 Confirmed reports: EUR 5.99
  • 3 Confirmed reports: EUR 16.99
  • 4 or more Confirmed reports: EUR 49.99

Prices are adjusted where required by app-store price tiers and shown before purchase. Your tier always applies to your side of a pairing, even if another user offers to cover both tickets. Inside the native app the app store bills you; on the web our payment provider does — we never see your card or bank details.

Withdrawal and refunds: see refunds and withdrawal. Nothing here limits your statutory consumer rights.

8. Conduct and what we do not do

We have zero tolerance for objectionable content, harassment, unlawful conduct, or abuse. You may not use paiir.me to harass, threaten, scam, impersonate others, or share sexualized or minor-related content.

We do not rank people by popularity or engagement. We do not sell or share your data for advertising. We do not show photos of you. We do not hold back a pairing when both people said yes.

9. Reporting, moderation and account states

Because we do not store avatars or chat, report evidence is sent from the reporting device at the moment of the report. You can report:

  • An avatar: non-consensual likeness or impersonation, apparent or sexualized minor, nudity or sexual content, hate/extremist or violent imagery, contact information in the avatar, or other.
  • A chat interaction: grooming or child exploitation, unwanted sexual content, harassment or hate, stalking or repeated unwanted contact, credible threat or coercion, scam or fraud, impersonation, or other.

Every report immediately creates a permanent pairwise block between the two accounts and goes to human review. The reporter receives a receipt only and never the outcome. If a human reviewer finds a report valid (an Accepted report), we notify the reported person with the reason, the action taken, and instructions to submit one appeal within 28 days. If an appeal reviewer upholds the finding (or the 28-day window expires without appeal), the report becomes Confirmed. Your lifetime count of Confirmed reports never decays and is visible and filterable in Candidates, Introductions and Pairings.

Account states and consequences:

  • Avatar reset: An Accepted avatar report resets your avatar to the default for future sharing (for an apparent or sexualized minor, existing copies are also replaced once Confirmed).
  • Suspended: Accepted reports for an apparent or sexualized minor in an avatar, or for grooming/child exploitation, stalking/repeated unwanted contact, or credible threat/coercion in chat, move your account from Active to Suspended. While Suspended, you can only submit an appeal and cannot delete your account; existing Introductions and Pairings stay visible with a suspension notice. A successful appeal restores your account to Active (the pairwise block with the reporter always remains).
  • Closed: If a suspension report is Confirmed, your account moves to Closed, its Introductions and Pairings are dissolved, the other person's consumed pairing ticket is returned, and your provider identity is blocked from registering again.

10. Ending your account

While your account is Active, you can delete it at any time in Settings (deletion is disabled while Suspended so moderation cannot be evaded by re-registering). Deleting an Active account removes your server row, Introductions, Pairings and chat state, while your used handle stays retired. If an account is Closed after moderation, we retain only the provider identity guard needed to prevent re-registration with that provider.

11. Changes to these terms

If we change these terms in a way that matters, we show you the change and ask you to accept the new version before you continue.
