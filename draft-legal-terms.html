Terms of use

1. Service, eligibility, and sign-in

paiir.me is a compatibility-screening service for adults. It does not predict chemistry and does not guarantee that you will meet anyone.

  • 18+ only: You must be at least 18 years old. We do not verify age; the declaration is yours, and we close accounts we learn are under 18.
  • Sign-in: You sign in via OpenID Connect (Google, Apple, Microsoft, Facebook, LinkedIn, or PayPal). We store only the provider and your anonymous provider ID — never your name, email address, or profile. One account belongs to one provider identity.

2. How paiir.me works (Setup, Candidates, Introductions, Pairings)

  • Setup: You pick a handle from our curated word lists (changeable at most once every 7 days; used handles are never reissued), choose our default avatar or generate one on your device from a portrait photo (real photos are never uploaded or sent; you can change or regenerate your avatar at any time), and answer a core questionnaire on your device, weighting how much each topic matters and marking red flags.
  • Candidates (free): Devices compare core questionnaire criteria and calculate a compatibility score. Users not excluded by a red flag you or they set appear as Candidates. You see the score and the topics you agree on (with the shared value); where you differ, you see only the topic name, never their answer. You can hide a Candidate with Not for me (a personal filter you can revert at any time).
  • Introductions (free): Selecting Introduce on a Candidate is private until both of you select it. Mutual Introduce opens an Introduction: avatars become visible, and you can ask optional questions (answering any question yourself first before asking it) and play visual Warm-ups.
  • Pairings (ticket required): Selecting paiir.me in an Introduction reserves one ticket on your side. Once both of you select paiir.me, you become a Pairing and unlock end-to-end encrypted plain-text chat (no images, attachments, or clickable links) plus optional conversation suggestions based on your Introduction.
  • Ending a connection and blocking: During an Introduction or Pairing, either side can end the connection at any time. Ending an Introduction or Pairing permanently blocks that pair — prohibiting any further contact or visibility between the two accounts. Blocking is only available during an Introduction or Pairing.

3. On-device storage and device-to-device transfer

  • No server backup: Your answers, source photo, and chat live on your device only. We keep no server copy and no backup; if you lose, reset, or break your device, your on-device data cannot be recovered and you start again.
  • Device-to-device transfer: When your device compares criteria or exchanges avatars, optional answers, Warm-ups, or chat messages with another user, the data is sent end-to-end encrypted between the two devices (directly peer-to-peer where possible, or transiently relayed in encrypted form). Our servers cannot read this data and never store a copy.

We have zero tolerance for objectionable content, harassment, unlawful conduct, or abuse. You may not:

  • include contact information in an avatar, use another person's likeness without permission, or redistribute another user's avatar outside the service;
  • depict a minor or sexualized content in an avatar; or
  • use chat to harass, stalk, threaten, coerce, scam, impersonate others, or send unwanted sexual or child-exploitation content.

5. Reporting, moderation, and account states

During an Introduction or Pairing, you can report an avatar (non-consensual likeness/impersonation, apparent or sexualized minor, nudity/sexual content, hate/extremist or violent imagery, contact information in the avatar, or other) or a chat interaction (grooming/child exploitation, unwanted sexual content, harassment/hate, stalking/repeated unwanted contact, credible threat/coercion, scam/fraud, impersonation, or other). Because we do not store avatars or chat, report evidence is sent from the reporting device when submitted.

  • Review, block, and appeal: Every report immediately ends the connection, permanently blocks that pair (neither account can contact or see the other again), and goes to human review. The reporter receives a receipt only, never the outcome. If a human reviewer finds a report valid (an Accepted report), we notify the reported user with the reason, the action taken, and instructions to submit one appeal within 28 days. If an appeal reviewer upholds the finding (or the 28-day window expires without appeal), the report becomes Confirmed.
  • Lifetime count: Your lifetime count of Confirmed reports never decays and is visible and filterable in Candidates, Introductions, and Pairings. We do not use expiring strike counters.
  • Avatar reset: An Accepted avatar report resets your avatar to the default for future sharing (for an apparent or sexualized minor, existing copies are also replaced once Confirmed).
  • Suspended: Accepted reports for an apparent or sexualized minor in an avatar, or for grooming/child exploitation, stalking/repeated unwanted contact, or credible threat/coercion in chat, move your account from Active to Suspended. While Suspended, you can only submit an appeal and cannot delete your account; existing Introductions and Pairings stay visible with a suspension notice. A successful appeal restores your account to Active (the pairwise block with the reporter always remains).
  • Closed: If a suspension report is Confirmed, your account moves to Closed, its Introductions and Pairings are dissolved, the other person's consumed pairing ticket is returned, and your provider identity is blocked from registering again.

6. Tickets, pricing, and refunds

  • Free monthly allowance: While your account has 0 Confirmed reports, you receive 3 free pairings per calendar month (resetting at 00:00 UTC on the 1st; unused free pairings do not roll over). After your first Confirmed report, free pairings permanently end.
  • Ticket reservation: Each Pairing requires one ticket per side. Neither ticket is consumed until both people select paiir.me; if the other person does not select paiir.me within 14 days, the reservation lapses and your ticket returns. Paid tickets never expire.
  • Ticket price tiers: To deter abuse, your ticket price depends on your lifetime count of Confirmed reports that did not result in account suspension:
    • 0 Confirmed reports: EUR 1 (base)
    • 1 Confirmed report: EUR 2
    • 2 Confirmed reports: EUR 6
    • 3 Confirmed reports: EUR 17
    • 4 or more Confirmed reports: EUR 50
    Your tier always applies to your side of a pairing, even if another user offers to cover both tickets.
  • Store pricing and changes: In the native apps, purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play and follow their respective pricing rules, currency conversions, and tax adjustments; if store pricing rules, tiers, or taxes change, in-app ticket prices may adjust accordingly. On the web, our payment provider processes the payment — we never see your card or bank details. The exact price for your account is always shown before purchase.
  • Withdrawal and refunds: See refunds and withdrawal. Nothing in these Terms limits your statutory consumer rights.

7. Deleting your account

While your account is Active, you can delete it at any time in Settings (deletion is disabled while Suspended so moderation cannot be evaded by re-registering).

  • What we delete: Deleting an Active account immediately deletes your server record, ends all open Introductions and Pairings, wipes your local app data on this device, and permanently retires your used handle. If an account is Closed after moderation, we retain only the provider identity guard (OIDC issuer and subject) needed to prevent re-registration with that provider.
  • Data on other users' devices: Because avatars, optional answers, Warm-up picks, and chat messages are transferred directly to the devices of your Introduction and Pairing partners, we cannot guarantee that copies already received on another user's device are deleted when you delete your account.
