Terms of use

Version 2 · draft · 2026-11-03
This is the whole agreement, written to be read. If any sentence here is unclear, that is a defect on our side — tell us and we will rewrite it.
Short version: you answer questions on your phone, we compare them without seeing them, and you only ever meet people you both chose. No photos, no hidden ranking, no data selling.

1. What paiir.me is

paiir.me helps adults find people whose expectations fit their own. It screens for compatibility. It does not predict chemistry and it does not promise you will meet anyone.

2. Your account

You sign in with an identity provider (Google, Apple, Microsoft, Facebook, LinkedIn or PayPal). We store the provider and your account number there — never your email address, name or profile. One account belongs to one provider identity.

You must be 18 or older. We do not verify age; the declaration is yours, and we close accounts we learn are under 18.

Your handle comes from our two word lists and can be changed at most once every 7 days. A handle that was used is never reissued.

3. Your answers

Your answers are stored only on your device, encrypted. We keep no copy and no backup. If you lose, reset or break the device, the answers are gone and you start again.

Your device compares your answers with someone else's. You learn how much overlaps and which topics you agree on, including the value you share; on topics where you differ you learn only the topic name, never their answer. Their full answer set is never shown.

4. Avatars

Your avatar is drawn on your device from a photo you choose, or you can use the default avatar. It is never a real photo, and we never hold it: it is sent directly between devices to people in Introductions and Pairings after mutual interest. You can change or regenerate your avatar at any time.

5. Candidates, Introductions and Pairings

Declaring interest in Candidates is private until both people declare one. Mutual interest opens an Introduction, where you can explore optional answers, warm-ups and conversation starters. Moving from an Introduction to a Pairing (with chat) requires both people to opt in and each to reserve a pairing ticket. Either side can end an Introduction or Pairing at any time.

6. Chat

Chat opens inside a Pairing once both sides opt in. It is end-to-end encrypted plain text only — no images, no attachments and no clickable links. We cannot read it and we do not store it.

7. Pairing tickets and pricing

While your account has zero Confirmed reports, three pairings a month are free and do not roll over (resetting at 00:00 UTC on the 1st of each calendar month). After the first Confirmed report, free pairings permanently end and never return.

A paid pairing requires one ticket on each side. Neither ticket is consumed until both people opt in; if the other person does not opt in within 14 days, the reservation lapses and your ticket returns. Paid tickets never expire.

To deter abuse, your ticket price depends on your lifetime count of Confirmed reports that did not result in account suspension: 0 = EUR 0.99 (base), 1 = EUR 1.99, 2 = EUR 5.99, 3 = EUR 16.99, 4 or more = EUR 49.99 (adjusted where required by app-store price tiers and shown before purchase). Your tier always applies to your side of a pairing, including when another user offers to cover both tickets. Inside the app the app store bills you; on the web a payment provider does — we never see your card details.

Withdrawal and refunds: see refunds and withdrawal. Deterrent ticket tiers and lifetime Confirmed report rules remain subject to legal review before launch. Nothing here limits your statutory consumer rights.

8. What we do not do

We do not rank people by popularity or engagement. We do not sell or share your data for advertising. We do not show photos of you. We do not suppress a pair when both people said yes. We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive conduct.

9. Reporting, moderation and account states

You can report an avatar (for non-consensual likeness or impersonation, apparent or sexualized minor, nudity or sexual content, hate or violent imagery, contact information in the avatar, or other) or a chat interaction (for grooming or child exploitation, unwanted sexual content, harassment or hate, stalking or repeated unwanted contact, credible threat or coercion, scam or fraud, impersonation, or other). Because we do not store avatars or chat, evidence comes from the reporting device at the moment of the report.

Every submitted report immediately creates a permanent pairwise block between the two accounts and goes to human review. The reporter receives a receipt only and never the moderation outcome. If a human reviewer deems a report valid (an Accepted report), we notify the reported person with the reason, the action taken, and instructions to submit one appeal within 28 days of the notice. If an appeal reviewer upholds the finding (or if the 28-day window passes without an appeal), the report becomes Confirmed. Each account's lifetime count of Confirmed reports never decays and is shown and filterable in Candidates, Introductions and Pairings.

Accepted avatar reports reset the avatar to the default for future sharing (and for an apparent or sexualized minor, also replace existing copies once Confirmed). Accepted reports for an apparent or sexualized minor in an avatar, or for grooming/child exploitation, stalking/repeated unwanted contact, or credible threat/coercion in chat, move the account from Active to Suspended. While Suspended, you can only submit an appeal and cannot delete your account; your existing Introductions and Pairings remain visible with a suspension notice. A successful appeal restores your account to Active (the pairwise block with the reporter always remains). If a suspension report is Confirmed or unappealed after 28 days, the account moves to Closed, its Introductions and Pairings are dissolved, the other person's consumed pairing ticket is returned, and your provider identity is blocked from registering again.

10. Ending your account

While your account is Active, you can delete it at any time in Settings (deletion is disabled while Suspended so moderation cannot be evaded by re-registering). Deleting an Active account removes your server row, Introductions, Pairings and chat state, while your used handle stays retired. If an account is Closed after moderation, we retain only the provider identity guard needed to prevent re-registration with that provider.

11. Changes to these terms

If we change these terms in a way that matters, we show you the change and ask you to accept the new version before you continue.
